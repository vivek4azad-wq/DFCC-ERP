Why 38°C / 43°C? • 38°C / 43°C क्यों?

These are not arbitrary demo values. They are the conservative Temperature Zone-IV assumptions used in CT-48 Annexure-I.

\(T_{max}-T_{min}=76^\circ C,\qquad T_{max}=T_m+38,\qquad T_{min}=T_m-38\)

CT-48 takes the permitted destressing-temperature range as \(t_d=T_m\) to \(T_m+5^\circ C\). It checks two separate worst-case cases:

CaseDerivationDesign ΔT
Maximum summer expansion\((T_m+38)-T_m\)38°C
Maximum winter contraction\((T_m+5)-(T_m-38)\)43°C

Important: these are two different critical design scenarios; they do not mean that one rail has two destressing temperatures simultaneously.

\(\Delta T_{summer}=T_{max}-t_d,\qquad \Delta T_{winter}=t_d-T_{min}\)

For an actual site check, use the applicable temperature zone and recorded/adopted \(t_d\). Keep 38°C and 43°C only when the CT-48 Zone-IV design assumptions apply.

हिंदी में: ये demo values नहीं हैं। Zone-IV की कुल rail-temperature range 76°C है, इसलिए \(T_m\) से अधिकतम और न्यूनतम temperature की दूरी 38°C होती है। Summer के critical case में \(t_d=T_m\), इसलिए rise 38°C है। Winter के critical case में \(t_d=T_m+5\), इसलिए fall \((T_m+5)-(T_m-38)=43°C\) है। ये दो अलग-अलग worst-case checks हैं, एक साथ लागू दो destressing temperatures नहीं। वास्तविक site calculation में applicable temperature zone और adopted/recorded \(t_d\) ही लें।

Source: RDSO Report No. CT-48 (Revised, November 2025), Annexure-I.
